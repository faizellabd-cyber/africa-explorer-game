🌐 Quick geography reminders

  • Africa is south of Europe and is bordered by the Atlantic Ocean to the west and the Indian Ocean to the east and southeast.
  • The Mediterranean Sea lies north of Africa. The Red Sea lies to the northeast, between Africa and the Arabian Peninsula.
  • A country is an area with its own government and defined territory. A border is the line that separates one country from another.
  • A landlocked country has no coastline on an ocean or sea. A coastal country has access to the sea.
  • Madagascar is both a country and a large island off the southeast coast of Africa. Zanzibar is an island region of Tanzania, not a separate country.
